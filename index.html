TarjimCall

Translated calls on any Android phone

Two people speak different languages. Each one talks normally — and hears the other's words translated, spoken aloud on their own phone.

How it works Privacy Policy

How it works

1

Call by phone number

Dial the other person's number. Their phone rings through a push notification, even if TarjimCall is closed.

2

Speak naturally

Your phone recognizes your speech on the device and translates it on the device. Nothing is uploaded as audio.

3

Hear the translation

The other phone shows the text in their language and speaks it aloud. Both directions work at the same time.

Built around privacy

🔒

End-to-end encryption

Recognized text is sealed with a fresh key generated per call. The relay server only ever sees ciphertext.

⚙

On-device processing

Speech recognition and translation run locally on your phone. No conversation audio is stored anywhere.

🌐

20 languages

The whole interface is translated into 20 languages, including full right-to-left support for Arabic.

📷

No ads, no tracking

No advertising SDKs and no analytics trackers are included in the app.

Availability

TarjimCall is in pre-release testing.
Store listings and a direct download link will be posted on this page. Both phones in a call need the app installed — TarjimCall does not translate ordinary cellular calls to a phone without it.
